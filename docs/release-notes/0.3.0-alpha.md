## What changed in 0.3.0

### Field-rotation correction

PIPP has no field-rotation correction. Its only rotation is the fixed 90/180/270 family, and every
alignment path (`-ctr`, surface-feature tracking, `-ctrf`, stabilisation) produces translation. A
rotating capture therefore smears with radius: on a 30-frame lunar sequence at 0.05°/frame,
translation-only stacking kept 26% of the single-frame edge sharpness; at 4°/frame it kept 2.6%.
That is the blurry limb reported after a minute of handheld lunar video.

Final-image integration now accepts an OPIPP extension:

```text
-derotate=on                 correct field rotation (default off)
-derotate_search=<deg>       search range either side of frame 0 (default 5, 0.1..45)
```

Both require `-stack=`. With the switch off, or absent, output is byte-identical to before.

The stage estimates each frame's angle against frame 0 by normalised cross-correlation on luma —
both images rotated by ±θ/2 so resampling does not favour either side, the full surviving rectangle
scored, 256 px working side, sub-pixel translation, and a predicted search window that widens once
at the edge. It then rotates with a bicubic Keys kernel (`a = -1.0`), crops every frame to one
centred rectangle all rotations can fill, and finishes with a correlation offset (below 0.05 px is
treated as zero). Mean, sum and median all work because the frames stay rectangular.

Measured on real lunar texture, outer-annulus Laplacian variance:

| Sequence | Translation-only | Derotated | Gain |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1.45° total rotation | 411.7 | 994.4 | 2.42× |
| 116° total rotation | 40.3 | 866.0 | 21.5× |
| 40 frames, 2.34° rotation **plus 5 px handheld drift** | 34.2 (`-ctr`: 268.4) | 813.3 mean, 828.0 median | 23.8× plain, 3.0× centred |
| identical stills | 1567.4 | 1567.4 | unchanged (diff 0.0000) |

Angle error averages 0.039°, max 0.13°. The mild 1.45° case is the weakest and misses an internal
2.5× target by 3%; realistic handheld rotation is larger, where the gain is an order of magnitude.

GUI: **Processing → Final image integration… → Correct field rotation (derotate)**, persisted as
`FINAL_IMAGE_DEROTATE` and `FINAL_IMAGE_DEROTATE_SEARCH`.

Limits: in-plane rotation only; the rotation must be observable (a featureless or perfectly
symmetric disc estimates 0°); `-derotate_search` bounds the search; cropping shrinks the canvas
(512² → 499² at 1.45°, 640×480 → 575×431 at 5°); raw Bayer frames are refused with `-db` guidance;
derotated median re-rotates per band and costs more than mean.

### Tests and docs

1,446 tests, 0 failing. New coverage: rotation estimation accuracy against analytic truth, crop
containment, bicubic-versus-bilinear margin, correlation offsets against a known shift, fast
rotation (4°/frame), still-sequence equivalence, CLI parsing and error taxonomy, GUI settings,
persistence and a form-to-engine run. `docs/derotation.md` records the verification, algorithm,
measurements and limits.
